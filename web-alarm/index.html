How It Works
  1. Your phone alarm rings at full volume. Volume keys, the power menu and restarting can't stop it.
  2. Get up, open this page on your PC and sign in with your ID and the one-time password on the alarm screen.
  3. Type the 12-digit code on your phone's keypad. The alarm stops.

Codes change every 2 minutes and use internet time, so a code noted the night before won't work in the morning.
