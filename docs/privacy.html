Privacy Policy

Editor Jisan Studio · StudioOS · Last updated 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Editor Jisan Studio ("we") handles information in StudioOS, the private production tool we use to manage our own client projects, and on payment.editorjisan.com.

Who uses StudioOS

StudioOS is an internal tool. It is operated only by Editor Jisan Studio and is not offered to the public.

Google user data

StudioOS connects to the studio's own Google Drive account to create project folders, upload finished videos, project backups and invoices, and share those folders with the client they belong to.

  • We access only the Google Drive files and folders that StudioOS creates or manages.
  • Google data is used only to deliver files to our clients. It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the studio's own computer and are never sent to any other server.
  • StudioOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Client information

We keep client names, email addresses, project details and invoices so we can deliver work and send invoices. This information is used only for that purpose.

Payment portal

payment.editorjisan.com shows payment instructions for our invoices. It does not collect card details. Access checks are done in your browser using one-way hashes.

Removing your data

You can ask us to delete your information, or revoke StudioOS's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
